# Execute and review one substantive cycle

After readiness, a cycle closes at **substantive Review**; searches, edits, and tool calls do not increment the shared counter.

## Stages

1. **Investigate:** Resolve only decision-changing unknowns with relevant evidence. Reuse valid prior research; challenge material assumptions before relying on them.
2. **Plan:** Choose the smallest in-scope work supported by evidence. Record prerequisites, affected targets, acceptance, and verification. A plan grants no operational permission.
3. **Work:** Follow the accepted plan and relevant specialist procedures without overriding their internal steps. Verify target, approval, safety, and rollback before consequential effects. Work may produce code, writing, research, analysis, or decisions.
4. **Review:** Check real outputs against scope, acceptance, evidence, and prior work. Challenge material weak points. Reconcile proposed findings as **supported**, **rejected**, or **unresolved**; a genuine failure does not prove its suggested diagnosis.
5. **Dispatch:** Increment the counter **once**. If acceptance is supported and no material gap remains, nominate [Close](close.md). Otherwise, if progress remains viable within the ceiling, resume from the **earliest stale prerequisite** (investigate, plan, or bounded work). For blockers or exhaustion, nominate closure with the true status.

## Guards

- An explicitly requested research-stage or plan-stage terminal may omit later stages but still needs Review. A research or planning **deliverable** normally remains Work.
- **Scope expansion is not automatic:** Review proposes, investigate validates need, plan covers the smallest change, and authority must pass **before** out-of-scope Work. Do not relax explicit exclusions.
- A separable material blocker may use [focus.md](focus.md), sharing the same budget and permissions. Another viewpoint alone does not require a child.
- For genuine material disagreements or review risk, **optionally use** [self-debate](../../self-debate/SKILL.md) or [self-review](../../self-review/SKILL.md). Apply their evidence and no-forced-findings gates; **do not invoke them merely** to fill perspectives, issues, or cycles. They never own outer scope, budget, or authority.
- Stop on acceptance, lack of credible information gain, or a real blocker. No ceremonial repeats, fabricated tests, or claimed independent verification without isolated evidence.
- Keep only material state and next action. Reuse unaffected context and checks; load optional tools and references only when the current decision requires them.
