# Execute and review one substantive cycle

Use only after [preparation](prepare.md) accepted the run and the [route index](INDEX.md) selected the active cycle. One cycle ends when a substantive **Review** has evaluated new evidence or a genuine attempt; intermediate tool calls do not increment the counter.

## Default sequence

1. **Investigate.** Resolve decision-changing uncertainties using the smallest relevant sources, contracts, or tests. Challenge material assumptions when needed. If prior investigation remains valid, reuse it rather than repeating it.
2. **Plan.** Derive the smallest authorized change or information-gathering step from evidence, with prerequisites, target, acceptance checks, and expected consequences. Plan coverage is not permission for execution.
3. **Work.** Perform only authorized actions covered by the current plan and scope. Delegate domain details to applicable specialist procedures; their internal order remains theirs. Work may produce code, writing, analysis, decisions, or research. Before consequential side effects, verify exact target, permissions, and rollback or approval requirements.
4. **Review.** Verify against the goal, current scope, acceptance, actual tool results, and previous work. Challenge plausible material defects and reconcile each finding as **supported**, **rejected**, or **unresolved** with concise evidence. Do not accept a suggested remedy merely because its reported failure is real.
5. **Dispatch.** Increment the shared cycle count **once** after substantive Review. If acceptance is supported with no material open gap, nominate [closure](close.md). If a gap can be closed within the ceiling, restart at the **earliest stale prerequisite**: investigation for missing evidence, planning for invalid approach, or work for a bounded fix. If authority, scope, or capability blocks progress, nominate closure with that blocker; at the ceiling, nominate handoff.

## Guardrails

- Explicitly requested stage-only outcomes may use **Investigate → Review** or **Investigate → Plan → Review**. Ordinary research or planning *deliverables* still count as Work within a goal-directed cycle.
- Scope expansion is **not automatic**: Review identifies the need; investigate the smallest boundary change, update the plan, and obtain applicable authority **before** acting outside the active scope. Never silently relax user exclusions or acceptance.
- New findings may justify a focused child through [focus.md](focus.md); children share the same count and restrictions. Another reviewer perspective is optional and not proof of independent verification unless its evidence path was genuinely isolated.
- Continue only when another substantive attempt has credible information or quality gain. Do not run ceremonial, no-op, or repeated cycles to fill the quota. Exhaustion or no viable resolution is reported, not disguised as success.
- Maintain only material evidence and next-step state. Reuse unaffected files and checks, batch independent low-risk reads when helpful, and expand context only on concrete need.
